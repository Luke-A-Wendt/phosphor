About the display model

A 60 Hz progressive raster with 192 active lines in a 262-line timing model. The renderer approximates scan-timed phosphor excitation, decay, beam spread, halation and a curved glass faceplate. Modern displays cannot reproduce an electron beam's timing or spectrum exactly.

This is a physically informed visual model, not a calibrated emulation of a particular tube. Glass reflections are procedural; they do not reflect your surroundings. No RGB mask is used on this monochrome display.
